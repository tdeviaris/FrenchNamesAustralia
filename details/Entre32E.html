Pointe Gicquel - Snug Point

Gicquel’s promotion to the rank of enseigne in February 1793 came just as the expedition was undertaking to complete the surveys of the D’Entrecasteaux Channel. The hydrographic work depended on comparing observations gathered by several officers: the tracks followed by the ships and boats, soundings, bearings and astronomical observations could then be cross-checked and incorporated into the charts prepared by Beautemps-Beaupré and Rossel.

The reconnaissances of February 1793, however, extended beyond the limits of the channel itself. On 20 February, Gicquel recorded the return of the large boat commanded by Willaumez, whose prolonged absence had caused concern. His party had continued the exploration beyond the entrance to the channel, towards Cape Pillar and the large bays of the east coast. The journal mentions, in particular, the reconnaissance of new stretches of coastline and the discovery of a river providing fresh water.

The refinement of the survey of the channel thus formed part of a broader undertaking: gradually extending knowledge of south-eastern Van Diemen’s Land by linking observations made from the frigates with those brought back by boats sent far from the main anchorages.
